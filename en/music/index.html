Every Neurepsic playlist opens with a warm, casual note left on the dining table or entryway by an early-returning roommate. Just like splitting utility bills or flipping a cassette tape, we want our music to feel naturally woven into your day.

01

Narrative & Lyrics Architecture

Never churned out with a simple prompt. Every track begins with defined narratives, character dynamics, period phrasing, and tailored lyrics.

02

Period & Genre Sound Design

We specify authentic instrumentations—from synth brass and drawbar organs to vintage 808s and jangly guitar riffs.

03

AI Generation & Strict Curation

From dozens of generations, human ears handpick only the tracks with melodic coherence, vocal emotion, and genuine musicality.

04

Dynamic & LUFS (-14 LUFS) Mastering

Carefully balanced for YouTube streaming standards, tuning vocal presence and instrument depth to listener-friendly loudness.

05

Painterly Artwork

Each video is paired with a distinct illustration evoking the atmosphere, light, and seasons of everyday life.
