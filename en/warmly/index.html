PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Your photos and chats
stay on your device

Photo understanding and AI responses run on your phone. Warmly does not upload your photos, captions, comments, or nickname to an AI server.

  • No photo or chat upload to an AI server
  • Core AI features work offline after initial setup
  • Permanently exclude any photo you do not want in the feed
